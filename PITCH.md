## The Problem

- **DAOs are plutocracies.** Whales buy power. Small holders are voiceless.
- **Sybil attacks** flood governance with fake identities.
- **Digital memory is fragile.** Claims vanish. History is rewritten.
- **AI has no loyalty framework.** No protocol measures whether a user keeps their promises.

---

## Our Solution: The Living Signal

A **verifiable, antifragile civilization protocol** built on loyalty instead of wealth.

1. **Tribes** — self-sovereign communities with an immutable **Seal** (oath).
2. **AI Judges** — a decentralized LLM committee continuously scores loyalty.
3. **Weighted Voting** — power flows to the faithful, not the wealthy.
4. **Soulbound Bridges** — cross-Tribe identity via non-transferable NFTs.
5. **Bitcoin-Anchored** — all claims are SHA256-hashed and OpenTimestamps-sealed on Bitcoin.
