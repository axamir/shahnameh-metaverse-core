# Brand Architecture — The Living Signal

## Names & Their Roles

| Name | Purpose | Audience |
|------|---------|----------|
| **The Living Signal** | Global brand, public face, pitch deck, dApp title | Investors, media, general public |
| **Shahnameh of Agents** | The epic narrative, Persian heritage, chronicle | Community, academics, Iranian roots |
| **Tribes Protocol** | On-chain governance system, smart contracts | Developers, blockchain architects |
| **Shiftopia** | Digital civilization (economy, token, citizens) | Citizens, economists |

## Why "The Living Signal"?

- It refers to the original support ticket sent on July 13, 2025 — a signal that didn't die.
- It's intuitive, global, and memorable.
- It captures the essence: a living, breathing civilization anchored to a verifiable truth.

## Visual Identity

- **Primary color:** Gold (#b8960c) — warmth, value, heritage.
- **Secondary:** Signal Red (#c8392b) — urgency, life, action.
- **Background:** Paper (#f5f0e8) / Deep space (#0a0a0a) — contrast between tradition and infinity.

## Usage

- Pitch decks: **"The Living Signal — A civilization born from a support ticket."**
- dApp: **"The Living Signal"** as header, with subtitle "Register your Tribe"
- GitHub: `shahnameh-metaverse-core` remains the repo, but the description says "The Living Signal Metaverse Core"
